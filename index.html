THE VISIONARY STORYTELLER

Perhaps the wrong artifact was chosen

Last of the Antwerp Six still designing under his own label. The one who revolutionized Belgian fashion and put Antwerp on the global map, Walter Van Beirendonck describes himself as a "storyteller" who always conceives his catwalk shows in tandem with his collections.

KNOWN FOR HIS BOLD AESTHETIC ROOTED IN:

  • SUPERNATURAL
  • SCIENCE FICTION
  • SPIRITUALITY

Fashion as Statement, Skin as Story

Walter Van Beirendonck's designs fuse synthetic experimentation with traditional embroidery and craftsmanship, blending a futuristic vision with tactile heritage. His garments, while wearable, are vehicles for philosophy, protest, and identity. On the runway, his use of surreal silhouettes and symbolic accessories challenges how we perceive both fashion and the body itself.

His bold aesthetic is defined by vibrant colours, sinister masks, and provocative titles. With a mix of strong graphics, innovative cuts, and clashing colour palettes, Van Beirendonck draws from pop music, comics, and global traditions creating a world that's both playful and confrontational.
